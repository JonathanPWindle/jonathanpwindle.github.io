xADA

Controllable and Expressive Audio-Driven Animation

Full face, tongue and head animation straight from speech, written directly to MetaHuman rig controls, with emotion detected from the voice or chosen by the animator. It ships in Unreal Engine as MetaHuman audio-driven animation and is still being improved.
