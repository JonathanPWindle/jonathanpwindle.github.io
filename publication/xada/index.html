xADA: Controllable and Expressive Audio-Driven Animation

SIGGRAPH Conference Papers '25 · August 2025

Read the paperAudio-driven animation docsWatch the Unreal Fest talk

Abstract

xADA is a model that generates expressive, realistic animation of the full face, tongue and head from speech audio. Audio features are extracted with a pre-trained Whisper encoder and decoded into animation by recurrent networks, driving MetaHuman rig controls directly. The model is conditioned on emotion, which can either be detected automatically from the voice or set by the user. In a user study, its output was often mistaken for ground-truth performance capture.

The model has shipped in Unreal Engine as MetaHuman audio-driven animation, and continues to be updated.

Cite

S. Taylor, S. Medina, J. Windle, E. Saez, and I. Matthews, “xADA: Controllable and Expressive Audio-Driven Animation,” in SIGGRAPH Conference Papers ’25, ACM, 2025. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1145/3721238.3730711.
